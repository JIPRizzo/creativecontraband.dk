COLLABORATE

Two ways to start.

Produce a portrait

A filmed conversation that shows who you are and what matters to you. I come to you with the cameras and lights, or we find a space that suits. We shape it together, and you receive a short documentary portrait to keep and share as you please.

Portraits can be a birthday gift, lessons for the people who come after you, a message to your future self, a video CV, or simply a way to find out how it feels to be interviewed in front of a camera.

Commission a story

For individuals and organizations who want to tell a story through film. We start with a conversation: why it matters, how best to say it, who it's for, and what they should feel.

ABOUT

The person behind the camera

I’m José Rizzo, an independent, self-taught filmmaker based in Copenhagen.

I’ve been crossing borders since I was 90 days old. Adapting was never a skill I had to learn. It was the starting point. With each relocation came more perspective, and an ever-growing fascination with what connects us across cultures.

After years hustling through commercial roles across industries, and accelerating my hair loss in the process, I chose to pursue filmmaking. It draws on everything I learned about communicating in business, but asks for different tools: showing more, saying less. One frame at a time.

Befriending strangers has always come naturally to me. It’s been my way of tending my roots while in motion. Interviewing connects me to that enduring feeling of belonging, not to a place but to a culture. A culture nurtured by curiosity, people and sharing stories. It keeps me young, receding hairline and all.

For businesses and organisations who want to trace their story's digital wake, a quality film is only part of the picture. We also agree on what success looks like, who we want to reach, and how we'll assess whether the story has landed.
